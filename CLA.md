# Ingle Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0, 21 November 2026

## What this means

- **You keep your copyright.** You give Skel Tech Ltd a licence to use your contribution. You don't transfer ownership.
- **Ingle stays open.** The core is AGPL-3.0 and free to self-host. The CLA doesn't change that for anyone.
- **Why it's needed.** Skel Tech Ltd also runs a paid hosted service, and add-ons that aren't open source. AGPL would otherwise require changes to your code in those to be published. The CLA lets Skel Tech Ltd license your contribution on other terms too.
- **Patents.** If your contribution is covered by a patent you hold, you licence that too, so nobody using Ingle can be sued over it.
- **You sign once.** You reply to a comment on your first pull request, and that covers all your future contributions.

This summary is for convenience. The agreement below is what counts.
