Where do I find this?
  • Google Calendar: on a computer, open Settings › choose the calendar › Integrate calendar › copy Secret address in iCal format.
  • iCloud: in the Calendar app, tap ⓘ next to the calendar, turn on Public Calendar and share the link.
  • Outlook: Settings › Calendar › Shared calendars › Publish a calendar › copy the ICS link.

Treat the address like a password: anyone with it can read the calendar. Ingle never shows it again after saving.
